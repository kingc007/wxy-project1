## Sample fetch record
- **Location chosen**: lat 40.6890, lng -73.9755 (Brooklyn), just south of Fort Greene Park. Rationale: the Parks Properties polygon (objectid 169827, signname "Fort Greene Park") has bbox lng -73.97768929523377..-73.97326832557873, lat 40.68973970523146..40.69333350354511 (from the query result), so the park's southern edge is ~80 m north of the point, inside the 180 m circle; the blocks between give a park-adjoining context for a "step down toward the park" brief. The point is my selection; I did not verify street names at that exact coordinate. Park `location` field: "Myrtle Ave., De Kalb Ave. bet. Washington Park and St. Edward's St."
- **Footprint query URL (exact)**: `https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/5zhs-2jue.geojson?$where=within_circle(the_geom,40.6890,-73.9755,180)&$limit=1000`  (fetched with curl -g)
