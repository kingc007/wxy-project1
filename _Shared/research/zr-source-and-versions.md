# NYC Zoning Resolution: official source, versions, City of Yes restructuring, bulk downloads, NYC Building Code source

## Question
(1) Official online source of the ZR text, URL patterns, page structure (server-rendered vs API), robots/terms. (2) Currency/last-amended date; City of Yes (Housing, Economic, Carbon Neutrality) adoption/effective dates; 2025-2026 amendments touching residence-district bulk. (3) Did City of Yes for Housing Opportunity renumber Article II Chapter 3 (structure only, no values). (4) Machine-readable/bulk downloads. (5) 2022 NYC Building Code official source and format.

Method note: HTML pages were fetched with WebFetch and with curl (saved raw HTML, parsed locally). Quotes marked [HTML] are exact strings from the raw page source (tags stripped). The nyc.gov DCP project pages (`www.nyc.gov/site/planning/plans/city-of-yes/...page`) returned a "This page has moved" notice to WebFetch and "Access Denied" to curl, so DCP project-page dates could NOT be fetched; the ZR site's own amendment-history data is used instead (it is DCP's official ZR site).
